Dealer confirmation — Topture, 1 Sep 2026

Topture answered the same question this packet is built around. They confirm the 240 V path and the GFCI prohibition. They did not name which interior wall — that still comes from the CL7G install manual: the heater wall, which is the back-wall center (fence side on this lot).

From Topture

The 240V feed doesn’t land on the heater directly. Your electrician runs it from the panel into a splashproof junction box mounted on the inside wall of the sauna room, no higher than 20 inches off the floor. The heater connects into that box at its base.

Nameplate on the 13 kW is 240V single phase — he sizes the breaker and wiring from there per NEC and local code.

One thing to flag for him: Saunum prohibits running this heater on a GFCI breaker. Easy default to fall into on an outdoor circuit.

Topture also sent the Air L manual we already host below: Air-L_10-15KW_4-25.pdf (same file as the PDF on this page).

The answer for the box location

There is no factory power stub. The CL7G is a wood kit. Per the install manual, the heater sits on the empty center of the back wall, between the left and right benches. The Saunum Air L needs a splashproof interior J-box on that same wall, max 20 in from the floor. House feed lands on the exterior of that back wall. Topture’s 1 Sep 2026 note matches this path; they left wall selection to the cabin layout.

Put it here — back wall

The solid back wall (fence side when glass faces the pool). That is where the heater sits, between the left and right benches.

Outside that wall: weatherproof enclosure with disconnect, AirIQ power unit, and the 90 W LED supply. Bias the box toward the house (right) so the trench is short. Near the coast use NEMA 4X stainless.

Inside that wall: splashproof J-box, max 20 in AFF, beside the heater. Heater cables are 5 ft. AirIQ power-unit cords are 16 ft.

Do not put it here

Not on the glass front wall (pool side). No chase through the glass.

Not on a side wall as the heater home. Benches occupy left and right; the heater belongs on the empty back-wall center.

Not high on the interior wall. Above 40 in inside the steam room, conductors must be 302 °F / 125 °C. Keep the heater feed low. AirIQ touch panel stays in the outdoor enclosure (IP20).

House panel → sauna

  1. Panel: dedicated 240 V 1-phase, 70 A two-pole, #6+N+G copper (90 °C). No GFCI on this circuit. Confirm spare capacity before pulling — 70 A is a real load.
  2. Run: from the house on the right, buried conduit to the sauna’s back-right corner (fence side). Hollywood / Broward electrical permit. Disconnect in sight of the equipment.
  3. Exterior enclosure on the back wall (heater wall): disconnect + AirIQ power unit (IP44, 2.4 × 10.1 × 6.5 in) + LED 90 W supply / RF driver. Touch panel (IP20) in the same dry box. Do not mount it on the neighbor’s vinyl fence.
  4. Penetrate low into the cabin. Interior splashproof J-box, max 20 in AFF. Heater bottom panel opens with two bolts underneath.
  5. Heater: rear against cladding, 2 in side/front clearance for the solid-steel body (8 in / 10 in if the open stainless cage is used). 20 in clear in front of the fan. Air outlet below the lower bench.
Do not connect the heater through a GFCI breaker. Stated twice in the Air L manual (Table 1 and Electrical Connections). Moisture in new elements can show leakage on insulation-resistance tests until the heater has been run a few times.

Site notes — Hollywood, FL

Permits

City of Hollywood Building Division: accessory structure (like a gazebo) plus a separate electrical permit. Broward is HVHZ. Disconnect in sight of equipment. Confirm glass/fenestration with plan review — the 8 mm tempered front is safety glass, not impact-rated hurricane glass.

Clearances from this Air L manual

Solid steel body: 2 in sides, 2 in front. Stainless cage body: 8 in sides, 10 in front. Fan output: 20 in. Ceiling to protective grille: 41 in on the 13 kW. Min ceiling height 79 in. Device rear may sit against cladding; only the rear.
