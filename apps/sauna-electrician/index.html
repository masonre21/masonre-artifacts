Where the electric lands on this sauna

SaunaLife CL7G cube + Saunum Air L 13 kW. The cabin has no factory knockout. Put the outdoor box on a solid side wall, and a splashproof junction box inside that same wall, no higher than 20 inches off the floor.

The answer for the box location

There is no factory power exit on the CL7G. It is a stave-built wood kit. The electrician chooses the wall. The Saunum Air L manual (your copy, Apr 2025, p. 33) requires a splashproof junction box on the sauna-room wall, maximum 20 inches from the floor, with the heater connected in a half-stationary position. Land house power on the exterior of that same wall.

Put it here

One solid side wall (left or right as you face the glass). Pick the side closer to the house panel.

Outside that wall: weatherproof enclosure with disconnect, AirIQ power unit, and the 90 W LED supply. Near the coast use NEMA 4X stainless.

Inside that wall: splashproof J-box, max 20 in AFF, beside/behind the heater. Heater cables in the package are 5 ft. AirIQ power-unit cords are 16 ft.

Do not put it here

Not on the glass front wall. The heater cannot mount there, and there is no chase through the glass.

Not high on the interior wall. Above 40 in inside the steam room, conductors must be 302 °F / 125 °C (SSJ-type). Keep the heater feed low.

Not the AirIQ touch panel or contactor in the steam room. Control panel is IP20 — outdoors it must sit in the enclosure.

Plan view — pick the side nearer the house feed

Interior 87.5 in W × 70.8 in D × 85.5 in H. Glass is the front. Benches on the back wall. Heater is floor-standing, about 18 in wide × 15 in deep, 73–94 in tall. Fan must blow under the benches. 20 in clear in front of the fan outlet.

House panel → sauna

  1. Panel: dedicated 240 V 1-phase, 70 A two-pole, #6+N+G copper (90 °C). No GFCI on this circuit. Confirm spare capacity before pulling — 70 A is a real load.
  2. Run: buried conduit to the sauna. Hollywood / Broward electrical permit. Disconnect in sight of the equipment.
  3. Exterior enclosure on the heater side wall: disconnect + AirIQ power unit (IP44, 2.4 × 10.1 × 6.5 in) + LED 90 W supply / RF driver. Touch panel (IP20) in the same dry box.
  4. Penetrate low into the cabin. Interior splashproof J-box, max 20 in AFF. Heater bottom panel opens with two bolts underneath.
  5. Heater: rear against cladding, 2 in side/front clearance for the solid-steel body (8 in / 10 in if the open stainless cage is used). 20 in clear in front of the fan. Air outlet below the lower bench.
Do not connect the heater through a GFCI breaker. Stated twice in the Air L manual (Table 1 and Electrical Connections). Moisture in new elements can show leakage on insulation-resistance tests until the heater has been run a few times.

Site notes — Hollywood, FL

Permits

City of Hollywood Building Division: accessory structure (like a gazebo) plus a separate electrical permit. Broward is HVHZ. Disconnect in sight of equipment. Confirm glass/fenestration with plan review — the 8 mm tempered front is safety glass, not impact-rated hurricane glass.

Clearances from this Air L manual

Solid steel body: 2 in sides, 2 in front. Stainless cage body: 8 in sides, 10 in front. Fan output: 20 in. Ceiling to protective grille: 41 in on the 13 kW. Min ceiling height 79 in. Device rear may sit against cladding; only the rear.
