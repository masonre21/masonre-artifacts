If you want extra legroom without Business

Cheaper comfort

Same two flights. Buy Standard/Flex economy (~$402 RT), then add Preferred or exit-row seats at booking or check-in.

  • Rouge A321 outbound: exit rows 18 / 31, or bulkhead row 12 — extra pitch vs 30″ coach.
  • A220 return: exit rows 12–13 are ~35″. Avoid the last few rows; they pinch.
  • Preferred seats are typically tens of dollars each way, not hundreds.
