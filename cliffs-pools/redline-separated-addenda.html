Surface removal: declined by homeowner

Upon inspection, the existing pool surface contains more than 10% hollow areas and consists of two existing layers of surface material. Based on these conditions, removal of the existing pool surface is recommended prior to installation of the new surface.

Estimated cost: $3,700.00 (first layer) + $1,700.00 (second layer) = $5,400.00 total.

The homeowner has been informed of the existing surface conditions, the recommended removal, and the associated costs. After reviewing this information, the homeowner has elected to decline removal of the existing pool surface and has authorized CPP to proceed without removing the existing layers.

Because removing only portions of the existing hollow areas may expose additional underlying hollow or compromised material and could potentially compromise the integrity of the existing surface, CPP will not remove or selectively address the existing hollow areas as part of the surface preparation.

Warranty effect of this decline (redline)

The homeowner understands and acknowledges that the new pool surface will be installed over the existing surface and that CPP cannot guarantee or warrant against future cracks, delamination, hollow areas, separation, or other surface-related issues that may result from beneath the existing surface.

The new pool surface will be installed over the existing surface. Homeowner accepts that CPP is not responsible for future cracks, delamination, hollow areas, separation, or other surface problems only to the extent those problems are caused by the pre-existing hollow areas and existing surface layers that Homeowner declined to have removed.

This Document B does not cancel, reduce, or void:

  • the original 2-year resurfacing labor / workmanship warranty for material failure of the new surface application;
  • the original 1-year warranty on new tile, coping, and new plumbing;
  • workmanship coverage on Document A ($1,550 pump station, related main-drain work, and tile float).

The original contract sentence about declining an addendum / change order (no warranties on “related items”) applies to this Document B only as limited above — it does not wipe warranties on the new work Cliff’s still performs.
